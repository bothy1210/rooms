How booking works

The system checks three things before a request can be submitted:

• Conflicts — the room must be free at that time (no double-booking).
• Capacity — the room must seat your expected attendance.
• Suitability — the room must be tagged for your purpose.

If any check fails, the system suggests alternative rooms that are free and large enough — from across the whole university. Requests for rooms owned by another department are routed to that department for approval.
