# User Guide — UZ Room Inventory & Usage Monitoring System

A short guide for the people who use the system day to day. It is organised by
what you can do; what you *see* depends on your role and your department/faculty.

---

## Signing in

Open the system in your browser and sign in with your **username** (given to you
when your account was added; capitals do not matter) and your password.
Your name and role appear top-right; **Logout** is beside them.

---

## The dashboard

The dashboard is your home page. At the top are the **scope buttons**:

- **My Department** — rooms owned by your department (the default for most staff)
- **My Faculty** — all rooms across your faculty
- **University-wide** — every room (planning offices and central administration)

Switching scope re-filters every figure on the page — the room counts, the
utilisation chart, upcoming bookings and pending requests. This keeps the
numbers relevant to you instead of showing the whole university at once.

---

## Finding and viewing rooms (everyone)

Go to **Rooms** to browse the inventory. Search by name, code or building, and
filter by building, type, status or minimum capacity. Click a room to open its
detail panel — capacity, equipment, accessibility, suitability tags and today's
schedule.

---

## Finding a venue by size (everyone)

Need a big room for a faculty meeting, a graduation or a large exam? Use
**Find a Venue**. Enter how many people you need to seat, the purpose, and
optionally a date and time. The system searches **every faculty and department**
and lists suitable, free rooms ranked by best fit. Rooms owned by another unit
are marked *cross-unit* — you can still request them; approval simply goes to
the owning office.

---

## Making a booking (everyone)

Go to **Bookings → New request**. Choose the room, purpose, date and time, and
expected attendance. As you fill it in, the system checks availability and warns
you immediately if:

- the room is already booked at that time (**double-booking prevented**),
- the room is too small for your attendance, or
- the room isn't suitable for that purpose.

When there's a clash it suggests alternative rooms that *are* free and big
enough. Submit when the check is green — your request is routed automatically to
the office responsible for that room.

---

## Updating room status (department & central administrators)

Open a room you are responsible for and use **Update usage status**
(Available, In use, Under maintenance, etc.). You can only change rooms in your
own scope; rooms owned by other units are read-only for you. Every change is
recorded in the audit trail.

---

## Approving bookings (administrators & approvers)

Go to **Approvals** to see requests waiting for your office. You only see
requests for rooms your unit owns. **Approve** or **Reject** each one — the
requester is notified automatically, and an approved booking appears on the
room's schedule.

---

## Registering a new room (administrators)

Go to **Register Room**. Complete the form and submit — the room enters as a
**Draft** and is *not yet bookable*. Central administration / estates then
verifies it and the system issues an official room code (e.g. UZ-LAB-101), at
which point it joins the inventory. This keeps room records accurate and free of
duplicates.

---

## Reports (administrators)

**Reports** shows utilisation by building and department, and the most- and
least-used rooms. You can export to **PDF** or **Excel** for planning meetings.

---

## Notifications & audit trail

The bell icon shows your notifications (booking submitted, approved, rejected,
reminders). Administrators can open the **Audit Trail** to see who changed what,
when, and the previous and new values.

---

## Roles at a glance

| Role | Can do |
|---|---|
| Read-Only Viewer | View rooms, availability and dashboards; submit booking requests |
| Departmental Administrator | The above + edit/register rooms and approve bookings **for their own department** |
| Approver | The above viewer actions + approve bookings for their office |
| Central Administrator | Everything, across all rooms, plus room verification and user management |
